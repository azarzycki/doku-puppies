Doku Puppies — privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026 · applies to the web game at azarzycki.github.io/doku-puppies and the Android app (package ca.zarzycki.dokupuppies).

Doku Puppies is a free puzzle game with no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics or advertising SDKs. This page explains the one kind of data it handles: your game progress.

What is stored

Your progress (levels finished, stars, streaks, treats, adopted pups, settings and any half-finished board) is saved on your own device. The same progress is also mirrored to a cloud backup so that it survives reinstalling the app and can be shared between your phone, tablet and TV.

The backup is filed under a random 128-bit key generated on your device. It contains no name, e-mail address, account, device identifier, location or any other personal information, and nothing in it identifies you.

Where it is stored

The cloud backup lives in Google Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC). Google processes connection data such as IP addresses to operate the service, as described in Firebase's privacy documentation. Transfers are encrypted (HTTPS).

Linking devices

"Link another device" in Settings creates a six-letter code that is valid for ten minutes. Entering it on a second device copies the same anonymous key there so both devices share one kennel. Codes are deleted after use or expiry.

Deleting your data

Reset progress on this device (Settings → Danger zone) wipes the local copy and starts a fresh kennel with a new key. To have a cloud backup removed, open an issue at github.com/azarzycki/doku-puppies/issues quoting the link code shown in Settings.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
